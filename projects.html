Networking Project

In Cisco Packet Tracer, I configured a small network by setting up IP addresses on the devices and connecting them with switches and routers. Then i confirmed connectivity by pinging devices.

Networking Project

I created a python game that teaches people about financial literarcy.The player makes choices and based on those choices that can earn or lose money and points. The goal is to make budgeting and good money habits easier to understand for all ages.

Event Management System
